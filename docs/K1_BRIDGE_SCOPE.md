# K=1 Bridge Scope

This document scopes the open K=1 bridge. The bridge has not been established
in `papasop/Geometric-Flow` and is not part of the frozen paper theorem.

| Stage | Objective | Status |

## Nonclaims

Normally attracting control tests do not establish K=1. Their Jacobian remains
full row rank, and observed attraction-tube failures may come from finite
control saturation rather than a K=1 critical rank drop.

Protected residuals, matter-like modes, and dimensionless constants are
speculative foundational horizons. They are not current repository claims.

## Stop Rules

- If the bridge has no spectral-gap closure, do not interpret it as a K=1
  implementation.
- Without a protected, uncontrollable, coordinate-invariant residual mode, do
  not use "matter emergence".
- If constants require tuning to match target values, do not call the result a
  fundamental constant derivation.
- Do not infer K=1 from geometric, control, or DAG terminology alone.

K=1 bridge work must use independent definitions and predeclared tests. It is
a bridge problem, not a semantic merger of the other tracks.
