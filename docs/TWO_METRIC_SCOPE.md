# Two-metric Geometry Scope

This document scopes the post-publication two-metric research track. It does
not modify the frozen paper theorem, and it makes no physical spacetime or
general relativity claim.

## Intended Separation

- `g_+`: a positive Riemannian metric for path length, optimization cost, and
  control cost on response fibres.
- `g_-`: a candidate pseudo-Riemannian metric for critical or null response
  directions.

The pseudo-Riemannian structure is candidate/open. No equivalence with physical
spacetime, Lorentzian geometry in nature, or general relativity is claimed.

## Stop Rules

- Do not infer a two-metric theorem from terminology shared with the control
  law or certificate-DAG track.
- Do not claim physical spacetime, GR equivalence, matter emergence, or
  constants from this track.
- A future theorem must define coordinate-invariant objects and prove the
  compatibility relation between `g_+` and `g_-`.

Geometry describes legal directions and costs. It does not by itself select a
controlled trajectory, prove DAG acyclicity, or establish a K=1 bridge.
